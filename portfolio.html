FixZa — Whangarei, New Zealand

Rescuing a Failing Campaign in 30 Days

Meta Ads · Paid Social · Pixel Infrastructure · Funnel Optimization

A home services startup entering the New Zealand market with no social presence, no pixel history, and a failing Meta campaign. I diagnosed the strategy, rebuilt it from scratch, installed and calibrated the Meta Pixel, restructured campaign objectives, and segmented audiences across two distinct customer profiles. Managed the entire campaign on NZD $582.69 and delivered results that exceeded industry benchmarks across every key metric.
