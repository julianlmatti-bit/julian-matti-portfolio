ELOS Thermal — Baltimore, MD

Building a Brand's Voice From Scratch

Influencer Infrastructure · Podcast Partnerships · Ambassador Program

Anthony Dragisic — former Division I soccer player at Villanova — built a product for cold-weather athletes. The brand had a story worth telling but no infrastructure to tell it at scale. I built the entire influencer program from scratch: sourcing criteria, tiered ambassador structure, compensation framework, and a Notion-based partnership database. One re-engaged podcast relationship produced a single clip that changed the trajectory of the brand's social presence. Zero ad spend.

FixZa — Whangarei, New Zealand

Rescuing a Failing Campaign in 30 Days

Meta Ads · Paid Social · Pixel Infrastructure · Funnel Optimization

A home services startup entering the New Zealand market with no social presence, no pixel history, and a failing Meta campaign. I diagnosed the strategy, rebuilt it from scratch, installed and calibrated the Meta Pixel, restructured campaign objectives, and segmented audiences across two distinct customer profiles. Managed the entire campaign on NZD $582.69 and delivered results that exceeded industry benchmarks across every key metric.
